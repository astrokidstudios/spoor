Trailz privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 (walk backups added)

Trailz is a hiking and trail-running app. It is built to work on your phone, without an account, and it does not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers. There are no ads and no tracking or analytics in the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location, routes you plan, trails you save and offline map areas are stored only on your phone. So are the walks you record, unless you sign in (see Accounts).
  • Your settings, such as your name for group walks and your emergency contact, are stored only on your phone.
  • Photos you take or add on a walk stay on your phone, unless you sign in (see Accounts).
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Your location

Trailz uses your location to show where you are on the map, find trails near you, record walks and help in an emergency. Your location only leaves your phone when you choose to share it:

  • Live location sharing. When you start sharing, your position is posted every few minutes to a private, randomly named channel on the free ntfy.sh service. Only people you send the link to can follow it. Sharing stops when you stop it.
  • Group walks. While you are on a group walk, your position and progress are posted to that walk's private ntfy.sh channel so the others on the walk can see you.
  • Low battery alert. If you are signed in and your battery runs low on a walk, Trailz saves your last position and battery level to your account so the people following you can see it.
  • SOS. The SOS screen only opens your phone's own call or message screen. Trailz does not send anything by itself.

Contacts

If you allow it, Trailz opens your contacts only so you can pick who to share your location with. It saves just the person you pick, on your phone, and never uploads your contacts.

Accounts (optional)

You only need an account for friends, following and invites to hikes. If you make one, Trailz stores your email address, username, display name, who you follow, and the hikes you start or are invited to. If you're signed in, your finished walks (with their routes) and their photos are also backed up to your account, so you don't lose them if you change phones or delete the app. Only you can see these backups. This is all stored with Supabase, our database provider. Your email address is never shown to other people. Others can see your username and display name.

Map and place services

To show maps, find places and plan routes, Trailz asks free outside services for what it needs. These include OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap map tiles, Overpass trail data (when an area isn't in Trailz's built-in trail map), Photon and Nominatim place search, BRouter route planning, Open-Meteo heights, iNaturalist wildlife information, and Wikipedia and Wikimedia descriptions and photos. These requests include the area you are looking at, but no account details. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Pictures

When you rebuild a trail from a picture, the text is read on your phone. The picture is not uploaded. Only the place names found on it are looked up.

Children

Trailz is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything stored with it, send a request through the feedback option in TestFlight or open an issue on GitHub. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
